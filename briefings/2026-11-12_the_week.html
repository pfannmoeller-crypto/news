The week in one story · follow-up edition, 9–12 November

Trust became the price of the AI boom

Nvidia's board met to decide whether its founder and chief executive can stay, after reports linked him to a woman US officials suspect of spying for China. In the same days Anthropic set the price for its stock-market listing, below the figures once floated. Investors are now pricing people and promises, not only chips and models; and higher-than-expected inflation keeps borrowing costs up while they do.

Three things that changed

1
Nvidia's leadership is in question. The board met on 11 November to decide whether to remove chief executive Jensen Huang; no decision was public at the time of writing. → Frontier Tech
2
Anthropic has a price. $70 to $78 a share, a value of up to $1.4 trillion; trading is due to start on 19 November. → Frontier Tech
3
Talks, but no breakthrough. Russia and Ukraine agreed a prisoner swap in Abu Dhabi; the US envoys went on to Moscow. → Geopolitics

Three dates ahead

13 Nov
Starship test flight 15. SpaceX aims to catch the upper stage for the first time; the permit for that was not confirmed on 12 November.
18–19 Nov
Anthropic prices its shares and starts trading. The largest stock-market listing of an AI company so far.
17 Dec
Next Bank of England decision. The US central bank's December meeting follows the same week; both are expected to lean toward higher rates.
