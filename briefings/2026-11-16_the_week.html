The week in one story · 12–16 November

The AI boom lost its best-known founder and kept its nerve

Nvidia's founder Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive after reports linked him to a businesswoman US officials suspect of working for China. Shares fell, then recovered most of the loss, and the US market ended the week higher. Demand for Anthropic's listing is reported to exceed the shares on offer. Meanwhile SpaceX caught an orbiting Starship for the first time, and Europe hardened its stance after a Russian-ordered sabotage attempt in Estonia.

Three things that changed

1
Nvidia has no founder at the top. Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November; finance chief Colette Kress leads in the meantime and the board is searching for a successor. → Frontier Tech, Markets
2
A rocket's upper stage came back to be caught. SpaceX's tower caught Starship's upper stage after 14 orbits; the booster was lost. → Muskverse
3
Pressure on both sides of the Ukraine talks. Washington floated NATO membership for Ukraine if it leaves Donbas; Estonia is closing its road crossings with Russia after an arson attempt it blames on Russian military intelligence. → Geopolitics

Three dates ahead

17 Nov
Nvidia's quarterly results. The first figures without its founder in charge.
18–19 Nov
Anthropic prices its shares and starts trading. Price range $70–78 a share.
19 Nov
Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen. The White House says Ukraine is not on the agenda.
